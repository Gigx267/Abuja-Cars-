Questions

Selling FAQs

How much commission do you charge for consignment?▼

Our consignment commission ranges from 5-8% depending on the vehicle value and services included. We provide a detailed proposal with all fees upfront — no hidden charges.

How long does it take to sell my vehicle?▼

Consignment vehicles typically sell within 7-14 days. Trade-in offers are provided within 24 hours and can be completed the same day.

How do you determine the value of my vehicle?▼

Our certified appraisers evaluate your vehicle based on market data, condition, mileage, service history, and current demand. We provide a transparent breakdown of the valuation.

What paperwork do I need to sell?▼

You'll need a valid means of identification, proof of ownership (vehicle registration or title), and service history records if available. Our team will guide you through the entire documentation process.

Can I sell my vehicle if I'm not in Abuja?▼

Absolutely. We work with sellers across Nigeria. Our team can arrange vehicle inspection, documentation, and logistics remotely or through our partner network.
